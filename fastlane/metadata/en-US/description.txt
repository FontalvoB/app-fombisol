PeopleNet is your company's mobile people-management platform. It brings together your performance indicators, your time-off requests and all your work information in one place — fast, clear and secure.

With PeopleNet you can:

• Real-time KPIs — track your performance indicators with clear, up-to-date charts.
• Permissions & requests — create and follow vacation, leave and medical-appointment requests; see their status instantly.
• Documents — access your work documents from anywhere.
• Evaluations — review your performance evaluations and history.
• Certificates — download your work certificates whenever you need them.
• Team — find colleagues by area and role.
• Account — manage your profile and personal information.

Built for teams that want less paperwork and more clarity: your requests, metrics and documents at your fingertips.

Access requires an account provided by your organization. All data is transmitted over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

Connecting people and possibilities.
