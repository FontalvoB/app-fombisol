PeopleNet is your organization's workforce management mobile app: evaluations, KPIs, requests and documents, all in one place.

• Evaluations — complete and review performance evaluations assigned to you.
• KPIs — track your performance indicators with clear, up-to-date results.
• Requests — submit and follow internal requests, no paperwork.
• Documents — access your documents and employment certificates.
• Profile — keep your information up to date.

PeopleNet is intended for employees of organizations that already use the PeopleNet platform. You need the credentials provided by your organization to sign in; public account registration is not offered.

Accounts are managed by the using organization: authorized staff create and manage access from the PeopleNet web platform, and data is processed securely with encryption in transit (HTTPS).

For access or support issues, contact your organization's PeopleNet administrator or email soporte@peoplenet.info.
